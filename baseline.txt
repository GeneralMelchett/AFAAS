========== RUN 01 ==========
You don’t have to clean the whole garage in one burst—just start, and let momentum help. Aim for **“usable and tidy,” not perfect**.

Right now:
1. Put on shoes and grab a trash bag.
2. Set a **10-minute timer** and clear only the obvious rubbish.
3. Then work in **25-minute rounds**: sort one area into *keep, donate, trash*; take a short break; repeat.
4. Finish by sweeping and putting the “keep” items where they belong.

Keep your phone away, and don’t get sidetracked organizing tiny things. **Start the timer now.** Three weeks of delay ends with one small action.

You only have to start with the first 15 minutes. You’ve got this.
